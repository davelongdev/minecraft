# Build a short, gentle Bed Wars natively

The best way to get Bed Wars into Game Forge is to **write a small native pack in a new `forge:bedwars` dimension rather than port an existing one**. Borrow proven techniques (and possibly map builds) from three open-source packs, JK's BedWars ToolPack (GPL-3.0, already pack format 121 on GitHub), ACT Bedwars (GPL-3.0, nine maps), and JuNi4/Bedwars (MIT). Every piece of the classic game has a clean vanilla 26.3 implementation. A per-tick `unless block … #minecraft:beds` check detects broken beds, adventure mode plus `can_place_on`/`can_break` items means only shop blocks can be broken, timed `summon item` at markers makes the generators, villager trades make a shop that needs no op and triggers no confirmation screen, and a pristine master dimension cloned back slice by slice resets the map. The real design work is fitting the game to the room. Hypixel's game runs up to about 50 minutes and lets a skilled rusher knock out a weak team in minute two, so the kids' version should be four teams of 3–5 on close islands, beds shielded for the first three minutes, a compressed 15-minute clock, no TNT or fireballs, and something worth doing for players who've been eliminated. Unlocking it by finishing Command Forge section 1 fits the motivation research if it's framed as readiness ("you know the commands now") rather than a price. If Forge Coins happen, they should buy cosmetics only, never power, and never feed a wealth leaderboard. Four things need an in-game test before the plan relies on them. Nobody has checked whether adventure-mode `can_break` breaks bump the `mined` stat, whether `/damage … by` awards kill credit, whether NoAI villagers still trade, or how free-summoned dragons behave outside the End.

## Classic Bed Wars is one rule plus a tuning layer

The whole game rests on one rule: while your bed stands, you respawn, and once it's broken, your next death is final. The last team with a living player wins ([Hypixel guide, 2020](https://hypixel.net/threads/guide-tool-an-updated-guide-on-bedwars.3617022/)). Everything else, the generators, the shop, the upgrades, and the timeline, is tuning layered on top. A caveat about sources comes first. **Hypixel closed its official wiki on 2026-07-21** ([Hypixel announcement](https://hypixel.net/threads/end-of-the-official-hypixel-wiki-july-2026.6112020/)), and the Fandom mirror refused automated fetches, so the numbers below come from forum guides written between 2017 and 2025. Those guides disagree in places, and the current live values aren't confirmed anywhere. That matters less than it sounds, because a kids' version should copy the *shape* of the balance, not Hypixel's exact numbers.

The four core modes are Solo, Doubles, 3v3v3v3, and 4v4v4v4 ([Hypixel overview, 2025](https://hypixel.net/threads/hypixel-bedwars-a-brief-overview.5969542/)), with 4 to 8 teams depending on the mode ([Hypixel guide, 2017](https://hypixel.net/threads/guide-bedwars-everything-you-need-to-know.1203017/)). For 10–20 kids, the 4-team layout of 3s and 4s fits directly. Iron and gold come from each team's own island forge, while diamonds come from **four diamond islands** and emeralds from the middle ([2017 guide](https://hypixel.net/threads/guide-bedwars-everything-you-need-to-know.1203017/)). That split, cheap resources at home and valuable ones in contested ground, is what pulls players off their islands and into each other. The most-cited diamond rhythm is **one every 30 s at tier I, 24 s at tier II, and 12 s at tier III**, with emeralds at 65/50/35 s in Solo/Doubles and 56/40/28 s in 3s/4s ([2017 guide](https://hypixel.net/threads/guide-bedwars-everything-you-need-to-know.1203017/)). Players reported a 23 s tier II in 2021 ([Hypixel thread, 2021](https://hypixel.net/threads/emerald-and-diamond-generators-speeds-depending-on-their-tier.4316840/)). No fetchable source gives base iron or gold rates, so we'll have to set those by playtest.

The shop ladder is the part most worth copying. Wool costs 4 iron for 16, end stone 24 iron for 12, and obsidian 4 emeralds for 4. Swords go stone at 10 iron, iron at 7 gold, and diamond at 3–4 emeralds. Armour is permanent: chainmail for 40 iron, iron for 12 gold, and diamond for 6 emeralds ([Dec 2020 guide](https://hypixel.net/threads/in-depth-bedwars-guide-for-beginners.3658536/), [Aug 2021 guide](https://hypixel.net/threads/guide-bedwars-item-prices-uses.4506178/)). Team upgrades cost diamonds: Sharpness 4, Protection 2/4/8/16, Forge 2/4/6/8, Heal Pool 1, and a trap queue at 1/2/4 in Solo/Doubles, roughly double in 3s/4s ([2021 guide](https://hypixel.net/threads/guide-bedwars-item-prices-uses.4506178/)). Traps are short debuffs that fire on the first intruder, such as blindness plus slowness for 8 s ([2020 guide](https://hypixel.net/threads/guide-tool-an-updated-guide-on-bedwars.3617022/)). Pickaxes and axes drop one tier on death ([Dec 2020 guide](https://hypixel.net/threads/in-depth-bedwars-guide-for-beginners.3658536/)).

The clock is the lever that matters most for a studio. Hypixel's 2020 timeline steps every six minutes: Diamond II at 5, Emerald II at 11, Diamond III at 17, and Emerald III at 23. All beds break at 29, sudden death with dragons comes at 39, and a tie lands around 49 ([2020 guide](https://hypixel.net/threads/guide-tool-an-updated-guide-on-bedwars.3617022/)). Plugin defaults land in the same range, about 44 minutes worst case ([CurseForge Bedwars Minigame](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/bukkit-plugins/bedwars-minigame)). That's a whole session. Hypixel's own fast variants show the genre takes big changes in stride. Rush starts with maxed generators and a pre-built bed defence, and Voidless is Rush without the void ([2025 overview](https://hypixel.net/threads/hypixel-bedwars-a-brief-overview.5969542/)). Those are close to the preset we want.

| Piece | Hypixel (forum figures) | Proposed Game Forge kids' version |
|---|---|---|
| Teams | 4 × 3–4 (3s/4s) | 4 teams of 3–5, teacher-balanced |
| Bed | Breakable from minute 0 | Shielded until 3:00, "shields down" title |
| Generators | Diamond 30/24/12 s; emerald 56/40/28 s (3s/4s) | Same ratios, two steps, faster base |
| Timeline | Tier-ups every ~6 min, beds break ~29 min, end ~49 min | Diamond II 4:00, Emerald II 6:00, Diamond III 8:00, beds break 10:00, sudden death 12:00, hard end 15:00 |
| Shop | 7 categories, ~40 items | 3 tiers each of blocks, swords, and armour, plus bow, golden apple, ladder, and water-free utility |
| Upgrades | 6 upgrades + 3-trap queue | Sharpness, Protection I–II, Forge I–II, one alarm trap |
| Destructive items | TNT, fireballs, Bed Bugs, golems | None in phase 1; a scripted "fake TNT" that only clears shop blocks later, if wanted |

## Three open packs are worth mining, none worth adopting whole

Only three vanilla packs are current. **JK's BedWars ToolPack (jkbw)** is the most complete. It has 10,017 Modrinth downloads, a GPL-3.0-or-later license, releases through 26.2, up to 8 teams and 64 players, a control panel for prices and phase timers, and experimental modes ([Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/datapack/jkbw)). Its GitHub `main` branch already declares `min_format: 121, max_format: 121` under a "v1.2.3 for MC26.3" README, though that build is unreleased and untested by us ([GitHub](https://github.com/JesKi13567/BedWars)). It ships **no maps**: you build a map and mark the beds and generators with its spawn eggs. **ACT Bedwars** is GPL-3.0, has a 26.2 build on Modrinth, and comes with **nine hand-built maps** shipped as structure files plus a lobby. It needs a companion resource pack, though, and its GitHub source stops at 1.21.11, format 94 ([Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/datapack/act-bedwars), [GitHub](https://github.com/atomic-cogwheel0/act-bedwars)). **JuNi4/Bedwars** is MIT-licensed, covers 1.21.6–1.21.11, ships 45 structure files across seven maps, and has a registry design: adding a shop item or map means adding a function to a tag ([GitHub](https://github.com/Juni4/Bedwars)). Everything else is pre-1.21, unfinished (LeeCom calls itself "unfinished and kind of broken" ([GitHub](https://github.com/LeeLux/LeeCom-BedWars))), or has no license.

| Pack | License | 26.3 status | Ships maps | Best thing to borrow |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| jkbw | GPL-3.0+ | `main` declares format 121 (unreleased) | No | Generator pile caps, `can_break`/`can_place_on` tags, "clean" reset by `fill … replace #tag` |
| ACT Bedwars | GPL-3.0 | 26.2 zip; source at 1.21.11 | 9 (GPL) | One file per team bed break, villager trade lists built from data, `place template` map loads into its own dimension |
| JuNi4/Bedwars | MIT | 1.21.11 | 7 (45 `.nbt`) | Registry/function-tag design for shop items and maps, bed loot tables emptied |
| LeeCom | CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 | Pre-1.21 rewrite | Separate repo | Ideas only |
| Unlicensed repos, PMC packs, Barrel Shops | All rights reserved | Mixed | Mixed | Read for ideas, don't copy |

Adopting jkbw whole looks tempting because it's already on format 121, but it fights Game Forge on every axis that matters. It runs in the overworld only, sets world-wide gamerules (`keep_inventory`, `immediate_respawn`, `locator_bar`, and others) that would leak into every other game in the hub, spans 657 macro-heavy files with comments in Chinese, and still leaves the map to build ([GitHub](https://github.com/JesKi13567/BedWars)). Pushing it through `port.py` is possible, but we'd own a large codebase we didn't write and can't easily teach from. Game Forge needs about a dozen mechanics, and they're short enough to write in our own conventions. That's the case for writing natively.

The packs agree on the techniques, which is reassuring. All three detect bed breaks with a `minecraft.mined:minecraft.<colour>_bed` stat plus a per-tick check that the block at a marker is still a bed. ACT restores the bed with "You can't break your own bed!" when the breaker is on that team ([ACT bed_broken/red](https://github.com/atomic-cogwheel0/act-bedwars/blob/main/datapack/bedwars/data/bw/function/bed_broken/red.mcfunction)). JuNi4 overrides the bed loot tables so beds drop nothing ([JuNi4 source](https://github.com/Juni4/Bedwars/tree/main/Bedwars/data)). jkbw spawns invulnerable item stacks and merges the pile into one stack once it reaches 12, and it hands ingots straight to an adventure-mode player standing within 2 blocks ([jkbw res/team/iron/1](https://github.com/JesKi13567/BedWars/blob/main/data/jkbw/function/state/1/res/team/iron/1.mcfunction)). ACT and JuNi4 run the shop as villager `Offers.Recipes` built from data ([ACT setvillagers_unified](https://github.com/atomic-cogwheel0/act-bedwars/blob/main/datapack/bedwars/data/bw/function/setvillagers_unified.mcfunction)). jkbw also gives every bought block and tool `can_place_on`/`can_break` against pack block tags, with those components hidden from the tooltip ([jkbw pack.mcmeta and tags](https://github.com/JesKi13567/BedWars/tree/main/data/jkbw/tags/block)).

Licensing is no obstacle for the studio. GPLv3 states that "mere interaction with a user through a computer network, with no transfer of a copy, is not conveying" ([GNU GPLv3](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-3.0.html)), so running modified GPL code on the studio box carries no obligations. The obligations start only if we hand zips to kids' laptops or publish them, and then the combined pack must stay GPL with source. MIT code just needs the copyright notice kept. ACT's maps sit under the same GPL as its code ([GitHub](https://github.com/atomic-cogwheel0/act-bedwars)), so one of its nine structures could serve as a first map while we build our own. Keeping a `CREDITS` file and the license text in the pack covers either case. This is a reading of the license text, not legal advice.

## Vanilla 26.3 covers every mechanic, but Game Forge's world-wide rules shape the code

The mechanics notes checked most of the syntax against the 26.3 client jar (format 121, build 2026-09-15). Below, "jar" means a fact was confirmed there, and "repo" means a pattern already works in one of our 26.3 packs.

**Beds.** Check every tick with `execute unless block <pos> #minecraft:beds` at each team's bed. That catches every cause of a broken bed (players, explosions, commands), and it lets the end-of-game timer break beds with a plain `setblock … air`. The `#minecraft:beds` tag lists all 16 colours (jar), but not 26.3's new straw bed ([Java Edition 26.3](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Java_Edition_26.3)), so the shop must never sell straw beds. Use the `minecraft.mined:minecraft.<colour>_bed` stat only to name the breaker in chat. The wiki says Blocks Mined counts only breaks with a tool that would make the block drop, and never in creative ([Statistics](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Statistics)). It says nothing about adventure-mode breaks through `can_break`. That's the first in-game test. If the stat stays at zero, credit the nearest enemy within 6 blocks instead. To protect a team's own bed, don't write any cancel logic: each team's tools simply leave its own bed colour out of their `can_break` list.

**Building limits.** Players stay in adventure mode, and the map is built only from block types the shop never sells. Shop blocks carry `can_place_on={blocks:"#forge:bw_build_on"}`, and tools carry `can_break` listing only shop blocks and enemy beds. Both components exist in 26.3 (jar) and take a block id, a `#tag`, or a list ([Data component format](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Data_component_format)). Our packs have already hit a gotcha here: 26.3's `#minecraft:dirt` doesn't include grass ([CLAUDE.md](../CLAUDE.md)), so write explicit lists in our own tags, and include every block a kid might click, because `can_place_on` tests the block clicked, not the empty cell. The payoff is big. Since nothing but shop blocks can ever be broken, the map itself can't be damaged, and a between-rounds reset is just `fill … air replace #forge:bw_shop_blocks` plus re-setting the beds. Bare hands can't break anything in adventure mode, so everyone keeps shears or a wooden pickaxe at all times.

**Respawn and final kills.** PvP Arena already does the hard part. It counts deaths with a `deathCount` objective and acts only once `@e[type=player,scores={…}]` matches, because `@e` picks living entities only and so waits for the Respawn press ([pvp-arena tick](../pvp-arena/data/arena/function/internal/tick.mcfunction)). Bed Wars adds a 5 s spectator countdown, then sends the player to the team's island, or keeps them in spectator if the bed is gone. `/spawnpoint` records a dimension (jar), so `execute in forge:bedwars run spawnpoint @s …` works. For void kills, `execute on attacker` exists (jar), and running `/damage @s 1000 minecraft:out_of_world by <attacker>` should credit the knocker. Whether that actually awards `playerKillCount` is the second in-game test. Game Forge sets `keep_inventory true` world-wide ([game-forge load](../game-forge/data/forge/function/internal/load.mcfunction)), so the pack has to strip iron, gold, diamonds, and emeralds on death itself. That also makes Hypixel's "resources go to the killer" easy to script. `immediate_respawn` is world-wide too, so leave the Respawn button alone.

**Generators.** Each generator is a marker carrying its own timer, interval, and cap scores. When the timer runs out, the marker resets it, reads `Item.count` of the nearest item (zero the scratch score first, since a failed `data get` leaves the old value), and runs `summon item` if the pile is under the cap. A team Forge upgrade just lowers the interval score on that team's markers. A dozen-odd markers cost almost nothing next to PvP Arena's piston work. The never-despawn `Age:-32768` value is borrowed from jkbw and prior knowledge, and the jar only shows an `INFINITE_LIFETIME` constant, so test it. jkbw's give-directly-to-the-player trick avoids the question entirely.

**Shops.** Villager trades are the right shop for kids. They run no command, so they never trigger the "Confirm Command Execution" screen that dogged NPC Forge ([CLAUDE.md](../CLAUDE.md)), and kids already know the trading screen. The 26.3 trade codec has `buy`, `buyB`, `sell`, `maxUses`, `rewardExp`, `xp`, `priceMultiplier`, `specialPrice`, and `demand`, and price items are `{id, count, components}` (jar). A sell item can carry `can_place_on`, `dyed_color`, and `unbreakable`. Trades are per villager, so each island gets its own team-coloured shopkeeper. Set `priceMultiplier:0.0f` and leave gossip at zero to keep prices fixed. Whether a `NoAI` villager still opens its trades is the third in-game test. Team upgrades and traps can't be trades, because they spend shared resources and change team state. Those go in an inline `multi_action` dialog whose buttons run `trigger bw_buy set <n>`, the permission-free pattern NPC Forge already ships ([npc-game show2](../npc-game/data/npc/function/internal/show2.mcfunction)). Inline dialogs also dodge the "dialog files need a rejoin" registry trap.

**Teams and the hub's colour map.** `/team modify` in 26.3 offers `friendlyFire`, `collisionRule`, `nametagVisibility`, and `seeFriendlyInvisibles` (jar). Team colour names are lowercase only since 26.2 ([Teaching Minecraft Java commands](Teaching%20Minecraft%20Java%20commands.md)). The catch is that Game Forge already ties sidebars to team colours (red = arena kills, blue = One Block lives, green = jumps, aqua = tut points, light_purple = NPC Forge, white = lobby), and its tick re-joins everyone in a game dimension to that game's team every tick ([game-forge tick](../game-forge/data/forge/function/internal/tick.mcfunction), [sidebars](../game-forge/data/forge/function/internal/sidebars.mcfunction)). Bed Wars needs the same exemption Command Forge gets, and its teams should use free colours such as `dark_red`, `dark_blue`, `dark_green`, and `gold`, each with `sidebar.team.<colour>` pointed at a `bw_status` objective. Wool and armour dye are independent of team colour, so the Red team can still wear bright red. Dyed leather armour with `dyed_color`, `unbreakable`, and Curse of Binding keeps the colours on.

**Reset.** 26.3's `clone` takes a source and a destination dimension and obeys `max_block_modifications` (jar), which Game Forge sets to 100000 ([game-forge load](../game-forge/data/forge/function/internal/load.mcfunction)). A hidden `forge:bedwars_master` dimension holding the pristine map can be cloned back in slices of under 100k blocks, spread over a few ticks, with both areas forceloaded only during the reset. This beats structure files for editing, because Dave builds the master once in game and avoids both the 48×48×48 structure-block limit ([Structure Block](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Structure_Block)) and the "redstone-pulsed saves live only in memory" trap Game Forge already hit. With adventure-mode protection, the full clone is a fallback and the cheap shop-block sweep handles most rounds.

**HUD and endgame.** Give each team-colour sidebar fake-player lines with `numberformat blank` ("Red ✔", "Blue ✘ 2 left"), a bossbar for the next phase, and an actionbar for respawn countdowns. Scope bossbar players with `distance=0..` inside `execute in forge:bedwars`, or hub players will see them. Win means exactly one team has a non-spectator member left, and a logged-out player should count as eliminated. Dragons are the fourth unknown. The ender dragon reads its fight origin and `mob_griefing` (jar), but nobody has tested a free-summoned one in a non-End dimension, so treat Hypixel's dragon sudden death as a stretch goal. A shrinking zone (particles plus `/damage`) or maxed generators is a kinder, all-command sudden death anyway.

Game Forge's world-wide rules apply here too: PvP on, fall damage off, and TNT, mob griefing, and projectile block-breaking off ([game-forge load](../game-forge/data/forge/function/internal/load.mcfunction)). Each of those is a design decision made for us, and mostly a welcome one. No TNT and no fall damage are kid-friendly defaults, and any fake TNT can be scripted to clear only shop blocks.

## Kids need a shorter game, a safety window, and something to do after elimination

Bed Wars is enormously popular with this age group. Roblox's BedWars has about **11.16 billion lifetime visits** ([RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/games/2619619496/bedwars)), and Hypixel peaked at **47,720 concurrent Bed Wars players** in 2021 ([2025 overview](https://hypixel.net/threads/hypixel-bedwars-a-brief-overview.5969542/)). Kids will arrive knowing the rules. Its complaints are just as well known. Servers advertise matchmaking because traditional Bed Wars "constantly match[es] new players against experienced sweats" ([BuiltByBit](https://builtbybit.com/threads/bedwars-hexabeds-investment.737857/)), and forum advice tells newcomers to practise bridging elsewhere first ([Pika forum](https://pika-network.net/goto/post?id=744782)). Roblox walls new accounts out of Ranked until they're 7 days old and level 10 ([Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/roblox-bedwars-ranked-guide)). A studio of 10–20 kids has no matchmaking pool, so the teacher's team-picking, fixed timers, and map design have to do that job.

No published classroom Bed Wars ruleset turned up. Minecraft Education offers a PvP toggle and teacher controls (pause, teleport, chat monitoring) but no PvP lesson ([Educator Toolbox](https://educator-tools.readthedocs.io/en/latest/Educator-Toolbox/)), so the kid rules below are design proposals, not evidence. A **3-minute bed shield** stops the minute-two wipeout that would end a weak team's whole round. A **15-minute hard end**, with a tiebreak on beds left, then final kills, then kills, fits a session with time to play twice. **Islands close together with short pre-built bridges** shrink the bridging skill wall. For the youngest groups, a **fixed kit mode** replaces the open shop. A **catch-up rule**, such as faster generators for a team whose bed just fell, keeps trailing teams in it. A **co-op "defend the beds from mobs" mode** maps straight onto the program's co-op board-game framing and gives kids who don't want PvP a way to play. The Newburgh Creates angle helps too. Teams, generators, a shop price list, and traps are tabletop ideas, so kids can design a paper Bed Wars variant (prices, island layout, a new trap) and then see it implemented.

Elimination is the genre's biggest cost for children. Board-game designers single out "the time eliminated players have to wait" as player elimination's most obvious problem ([Tabletop Games Blog](https://tabletopgamesblog.com/2022/09/13/slow-death-player-elimination-in-board-games-topic-discussion)), and elimination has fallen out of favour partly because knocking friends out feels bad for the knocker too ([League of Game Makers](https://www.leagueofgamemakers.com/game-elements-elimination/)). The fixes are short games, a choice of what to do next ([BGA request](https://ca.boardgamearena.com/bug?id=30375)), and a way to stay involved ([There Will Be Games](https://therewillbe.games/articles-essays/9111-slow-death-player-elimination-in-board-games)). In Minecraft, that means spectator stands like PvP Arena's (Resistance V, back to the stands on death) with a "ghost helper" job: an eliminated kid could send one iron to their team each minute or ring the team's alarm. The 15-minute cap does most of the work, since nobody waits longer than a few minutes.

Teacher controls follow the existing `___` convention, op-only: `___bw_start`, `___bw_stop`, `___bw_pause` (freeze the clock and the players), `___bw_teams` (auto-balance using Command Forge points or past wins), `___bw_move`, `___bw_out` (send a player to the stands or the hub), `___bw_shield {minutes}` to extend protection live, and `___bw_end`. Vanilla has no per-player mute, and nobody checked whether 26.3 has a chat gamerule. In a LAN studio, the adult in the room is the real moderation tool.

## Gate on finishing the tutorial as readiness, and keep coins cosmetic

The motivation research supports gating, but only a certain kind. Deci, Koestner and Ryan's 1999 meta-analysis found that expected tangible rewards substantially undermine intrinsic motivation ([Behavioral Economics](https://www.behavioraleconomics.com/resources/mini-encyclopedia-of-be/overjustification-effect)). Cameron and Pierce found the effect minimal outside specific conditions ([Springer](https://link.springer.com/doi/10.1007/BF03392017)), and the debate continues. Gamification overall shows small positive effects: g = .49 cognitive, .36 motivational, and .25 behavioral, with only the cognitive effect stable in rigorous studies ([Sailer & Homner 2020](https://opus.bibliothek.uni-augsburg.de/opus4/frontdoor/index/index/docId/109056)). In programming education, the d = 0.4 effect was "essentially due to the use of levels", while badges, points, and leaderboards showed no significant effect ([ISCTE meta-analysis](https://repositorio.iscte-iul.pt/bitstream/10071/29213/1/article_95533.pdf)). Leaderboards plus badges actually lowered motivation and exam scores over a 16-week course ([Hanus & Fox 2015 summary](https://www.smhp.psych.ucla.edu/pdfdocs/gamil.pdf)). The cautionary tale for currencies is Prodigy. Its FTC complaint counted **16 membership ads and 4 math problems in 19 minutes**, plus visible classroom inequity where members "sail around on a cloud, while non-members literally tromp" ([Axios](https://www.axios.com/2021/02/19/prodigy-math-game-ftc-complaint), [Fairplay complaint](https://fairplayforkids.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/02/Prodigy_Complaint_Feb21.pdf)).

So the gate should be a **level, not a price**. Bed Wars unlocks when `forge_s1` (Command Forge section 1 finished, a score Game Forge already keeps ([CLAUDE.md](../CLAUDE.md))) is set, and the unlock reads as competence feedback: "You've learned /give, /tp, and /effect, so you're ready for Bed Wars." The Bed Wars NPC at the hub should explain what's still to do rather than just refuse. Make the unlock a celebration moment (title, firework, the NPC's dialog changing), which also keeps it closer to the less-damaging unexpected reward than to an announced pay rate. Even better, make the learning *part of the game* instead of just the ticket. Later phases can add a command-block trap a team writes itself, or a `/trigger` shop line, so commands become a Bed Wars skill. Keep a teacher override (`___bw_unlock`), because shutting one kid out of the group game on game day is a real social cost in a room of 15.

If Forge Coins happen, earn them across all of Game Forge (quests, wins, helping), spend them only on cosmetics such as a victory firework, a team banner, or a trail particle, and never show a coin-wealth leaderboard. Anything that buys power in Bed Wars recreates Prodigy's "members fly, others walk" problem, where everyone in a shared studio can see it.

## A five-phase build from playable core to teacher polish

Each phase ends with something the kids can play, and the four unknowns get tested in phase 1 before anything is built on them.

| Phase | Scope | Exit test |
|---|---|---|
| 0. Spike (an evening) | `forge:bedwars` + `forge:bedwars_master` via `gen_dims.py`; a bare two-island map; test whether adventure `can_break` bumps `mined`, whether `/damage … by` gives `playerKillCount`, whether NoAI villagers trade, and whether `Age:-32768` stops despawning | Four yes/no answers written into `CLAUDE.md` |
| 1. Core loop | 4 teams on free colours, Game Forge team-join exemption, bed check + break broadcast, adventure + `can_place_on`/`can_break` tags, respawn/final kill/win, iron/gold forges, one villager shop per island (3 tiers), shop-block sweep reset, `___bw_start`/`___bw_stop` | A 4-team round plays start to finish twice in one session |
| 2. Kid rules and clock | Bed shield, compressed phase timer with bossbar, diamond/emerald islands with tiers, beds break at 10:00, hard end at 15:00 with tiebreak, sidebars | Rounds reliably end in ≤ 15 min |
| 3. Engagement | Spectator stands with a ghost-helper action, upgrades/trap dialog via `/trigger bw_buy`, catch-up rule, fixed-kit mode, co-op mob mode | Eliminated kids stay in the room and engaged |
| 4. Hub integration | Bed Wars lobby NPC gated on `forge_s1` with readiness text and unlock celebration, `___bw_unlock`, `___bw_teams` balancing, master-dimension clone reset, optional cosmetic Forge Coins | A new kid who just finished section 1 walks from the hub into a game |

Write the pack natively in the `forge` namespace with `distance=0..` selectors and no gamerule lines, so `port.py` never needs to touch it. Generate the map's tags, marker positions, and shop trades from a small Python table, as `gen_npcs.py` and `gen_stadium.py` do, so prices and timers live in one editable place. Build the map in the master dimension on the creative PvP Arena Build server or in place, and keep it 64–96 blocks across so clone slices stay small.

## Conclusion

The research shifts where the hard part lives. Every Bed Wars mechanic has a known vanilla answer, and the open packs agree on it, so the code isn't the challenge. The real design question is what happens to a nine-year-old whose bed falls at minute two, and every kid-specific choice above (shield, clock, close islands, stands, co-op mode) answers it. One structural insight runs through the whole plan: adventure mode plus item-component permissions is both the protection system and the reset system, because a map that can't be broken never needs rebuilding. That makes it the single most important early decision.

The unlock deserves the same care as the game. Tying Bed Wars to Command Forge works best when it feels like graduating rather than paying, and it works better still when commands become something kids *use* in Bed Wars, a self-written trap or a scripted upgrade, rather than a toll they paid once. Treat Forge Coins as an optional cosmetic layer to add after phase 4, if the kids ask for one, not as the engine of motivation.
