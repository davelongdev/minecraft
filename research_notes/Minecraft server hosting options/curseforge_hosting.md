# CurseForge server hosting (CurseForge Servers beta + the BisectHosting partner program) for a kids' Minecraft 26.3 / Fabric program

Research date: 2026-10-18. Scope: CurseForge-branded hosting only (first-party "CurseForge Servers" beta, operated by Shockbyte, and the older BisectHosting affiliation program). Anything dated before mid-2026 is flagged as possibly stale.

Headline for the teacher: "CurseForge Servers" is a brand-new (8 Sep 2026) beta that only hosts servers created from an existing CurseForge modpack that already has a server pack; custom modpacks, plan changes, longer billing periods, and website-side creation are all "coming." It is technically a Shockbyte server (Shockbyte control panel, console, files, backups) billed by Tebex at $12/$24/$36 per month for 4/8/12 GB. For a program that needs several custom Fabric 26.3 servers at once, it is the wrong front door today: each server is a separate $12+ subscription, nothing in the flow is built for custom jars or datapacks, and the buyer must be 16+. The underlying Shockbyte product (or BisectHosting direct) is the realistic version of this option.

## Q1. Who operates "CurseForge servers" in 2026, and what are the plans, prices, specs, regions, and pause/stop options?

### Takeaway
Since 8 Sep 2026 there is a first-party "CurseForge Servers (BETA)" inside the CurseForge app: Shockbyte provisions and runs the server, Tebex is the seller/merchant of record, and CurseForge/Overwolf takes a cut and pays 20% of net income to modpack and mod authors. Three monthly plans: Iron $12 (4 GB, 2 shared CPUs), Gold $24 (8 GB, 3 shared CPUs), Diamond $36 (12 GB, 4 shared CPUs). The older, separate "CurseForge Server Affiliation Program" with BisectHosting is a referral/discount scheme, not a CurseForge-run host.

### Cited Findings
- CurseForge announced "CurseForge Servers (BETA)" on 8 Sep 2026 ("Today we are starting the rollout of CurseForge Servers (BETA)"); it rolls out gradually, so the Servers tab may not appear for every user yet; Shockbyte is named as the hosting partner. — [CurseForge blog, 2026-09-08](https://blog.curseforge.com/the-easiest-way-to-play-modded-minecraft-with-your-friends-curseforge-servers-are-here/)
- Revenue share: "20% of net income" goes to the modpack author and the authors of the mods in that pack through the existing Rewards program, automatically from day one. — [CurseForge blog, 2026-09-08](https://blog.curseforge.com/the-easiest-way-to-play-modded-minecraft-with-your-friends-curseforge-servers-are-here/)
- Official support article (modified Tue 8 Sep 2026): Shockbyte hosts and provisions the server; Tebex handles billing and "is the seller and merchant of record," not CurseForge or Overwolf; billing contact is checkout-support@tebex.io. — [CurseForge support: Setting Up a CurseForge Minecraft Server](https://support.curseforge.com/support/solutions/articles/9000278741-setting-up-a-curseforge-minecraft-server)
- Plans (all billed monthly), per the same support article: Iron $12 = 4 GB RAM, 2 shared CPUs, "1–5 players, light mod count"; Gold $24 = 8 GB, 3 shared CPUs, "6–15 players, multiple mods"; Diamond $36 = 12 GB, 4 shared CPUs, "16–25 players, multiple mods"; heavier packs may need Gold or Diamond "even with fewer players." — [CurseForge support](https://support.curseforge.com/support/solutions/articles/9000278741-setting-up-a-curseforge-minecraft-server)
- The same three prices are repeated by third parties: $12/4 GB, $24/8 GB, $36/12 GB, "powered by Shockbyte and billed through Tebex." — [iTechGuides, 2026-10-15](https://www.itechguides.com/how-much-does-an-rlcraft-server-cost-in-2026/)
- Regions: the purchase flow shows "up to 9" regions sorted by estimated ping, with the lowest-ping one marked "Suggested"; the article does not name the regions. — [CurseForge support](https://support.curseforge.com/support/solutions/articles/9000278741-setting-up-a-curseforge-minecraft-server)
- Plan changes are not available in the beta: "changing a server plan is not supported during the beta, so choose the size that fits your group now. It is one of the first things we are adding." Custom plans and "discounted longer-period plans" are "coming soon." — [CurseForge blog, 2026-09-08](https://blog.curseforge.com/the-easiest-way-to-play-modded-minecraft-with-your-friends-curseforge-servers-are-here/)
- Cancellation/deletion is done through Tebex: "Manage subscription" → "Manage Plan"; refunds, plan changes and failed payments are Tebex's responsibility. — [CurseForge support](https://support.curseforge.com/support/solutions/articles/9000278741-setting-up-a-curseforge-minecraft-server)
- Tebex buyer terms: subscriptions can be cancelled from any Tebex email or at checkout.tebex.io/payment-history; "Upon cancellation, no further subscription payments will be taken"; "Except where required by applicable consumer law, this transaction is final there are no refunds" (EU buyers get a 14-day withdrawal right). — [Tebex Terms & Conditions](https://checkout.tebex.io/terms)
- Storage, player-slot caps, and the number of servers per account are not stated in the support article (it covers none of: refund terms, pausing, age, servers per account, storage, SFTP, whitelist, backup limits). — [CurseForge support](https://support.curseforge.com/support/solutions/articles/9000278741-setting-up-a-curseforge-minecraft-server)
- The separate, older program: "The CurseForge Affiliation Program is a collaboration between BisectHosting and CurseForge designed to reward authors" with CurseForge reward points when users buy BisectHosting via affiliate links; opt-in through the Author Console; "only Minecraft Modpacks are supported." — [CurseForge support: Server Affiliation Program FAQ](https://support.curseforge.com/support/solutions/articles/9000240602-curseforge-server-affiliation-program-faq)
- BisectHosting's side of that partnership (possibly stale, undated blog): new customers get 25% off the first month via a creator link, creators earn a commission; BisectHosting cites 24/7 support, free DDoS protection and 20 hosting locations. — [BisectHosting blog](https://bisecthosting.com/blog/bisect-hosting-curseforge-partnership-server-discount-creator-kickback)
- The first-party service has drawn creator backlash (Sept 2026): modpack authors worry it undercuts hosting-partner deals that pay "around 20%" and some asked for an opt-out; the article notes the service is still "in beta development." — [GamingHQ, 2026-09-17](https://gaminghq.eu/2026/09/17/curseforge-servers-minecraft-modpack-creator-backlash/)
- Shockbyte's own direct plans (Feb 2026 review, possibly stale) are priced per RAM tier: 4 GB "Iron" $15.99/mo base, 8 GB "Emerald" $31.99/mo, 12 GB "Zeus" $47.99/mo, 16 GB "Titan" $63.99/mo (30% first-month promos lower these). — [minecraft-hosting.top, 2026-02-13](https://minecraft-hosting.top/2026/02/13/shockbyte-minecraft-hosting/)

### Inferences
- The CurseForge price list ($12/4 GB, $24/8 GB, $36/12 GB = $3.00/GB) is noticeably cheaper than Shockbyte's published direct prices in the Feb 2026 review ($15.99/4 GB, $31.99/8 GB, $47.99/12 GB), so CurseForge's beta is effectively a discounted Shockbyte plan; whether that holds after beta is unknown.
- "Shared CPUs" is Shockbyte wording for vCPU shares on a shared node; there is no dedicated-core option in the beta.
- There is no pause/suspend option: billing is a monthly Tebex subscription that you either keep or cancel. Cancelling stops renewals; no source describes prorated refunds or keeping files after cancellation.
- "US East" is almost certainly offered (Shockbyte has long run North American locations), but the beta's region names are not published; a teacher should check the region dropdown before purchase.

### Gaps
- Exact list of the "up to 9" regions (not published; shown only in-app).
- Per-plan disk storage and player-slot hard caps (none stated; Shockbyte generally advertises no fixed slot limits).
- Whether CurseForge takes a margin above Shockbyte's price, and what the price is after beta.
- Whether the Overwolf/CurseForge terms or Tebex store page impose a servers-per-account limit.

## Q2. Does it support Minecraft 26.3 and Fabric Loader? Can you upload your own mod jars and datapacks (file manager/SFTP), or is it limited to CurseForge-listed modpacks?

### Takeaway
At purchase time the beta is limited to CurseForge modpacks that already have a server pack (~9000 of them); custom/own modpacks and vanilla/Fabric "from scratch" are explicitly future features. After purchase, however, the server is a normal Shockbyte server managed in the Shockbyte control panel (console, files, backups), and Shockbyte's panel supports Fabric instances, custom jars and SFTP, so repurposing is technically possible but unsupported by CurseForge's flow and not described anywhere official.

### Cited Findings
- "At launch, only modpacks that already have a serverpack on CurseForge" can be used; support for all public modpacks and custom modpacks is planned. — [CurseForge blog, 2026-09-08](https://blog.curseforge.com/the-easiest-way-to-play-modded-minecraft-with-your-friends-curseforge-servers-are-here/)
- "Currently, there are ~9000 modpacks supported for server creation. Support for any modpack, including your own custom ones, will be added to future versions." The modpack card shows "Using: [filename]_server.zip" for the exact server pack to be deployed. — [CurseForge support](https://support.curseforge.com/support/solutions/articles/9000278741-setting-up-a-curseforge-minecraft-server)
- The in-app "Join" button (launches the CurseForge client into the server) is "only available for game versions above 1.20.1"; no other version constraints are listed, and loaders (Fabric/NeoForge) are not named. — [CurseForge support](https://support.curseforge.com/support/solutions/articles/9000278741-setting-up-a-curseforge-minecraft-server)
- "Manage server" opens the Shockbyte control panel, described as including console, files, restart and backups; "Get Support" routes to Shockbyte. — [CurseForge support](https://support.curseforge.com/support/solutions/articles/9000278741-setting-up-a-curseforge-minecraft-server)
- Shockbyte panel, Fabric: stop the server, open the Instances tab, create a new instance, choose Fabric and a game version, then "Switch"; "The Shockbyte Panel lets owners store up to 3 unique server instances by default." — [Shockbyte KB: How to Install Fabric on Your Minecraft Server](https://shockbyte.com/help/knowledgebase/articles/how-to-install-fabric-on-your-minecraft-server) (fetch returned 403; wording from search snippet)
- Shockbyte panel, custom jars: "it is possible to install any server type or modpack on your Shockbyte server. To do this, you will need to install it as a custom JAR" (article dated Oct 2024, possibly stale). — [Shockbyte KB: custom JAR](https://shockbyte.com/help/knowledgebase/articles/how-to-install-a-custom-jar-custom-server-type)
- Shockbyte panel, SFTP: the Files tab has "SFTP Connect" for FileZilla/WinSCP; the web file manager can zip and download folders; connection details are under Files → SFTP Connect. — [Shockbyte KB: manual backup](https://shockbyte.com/billing/knowledgebase/44/How-to-Manually-Backup-Your-Server.html)
- Shockbyte publishes an "upload an existing world" guide that the CurseForge blog links to for moving worlds onto a CurseForge Server. — [CurseForge blog, 2026-09-08](https://blog.curseforge.com/the-easiest-way-to-play-modded-minecraft-with-your-friends-curseforge-servers-are-here/)
- Minecraft 26.x servers require Java 25 (Mojang per-version metadata and PaperMC requirements); a 26.x jar on Java 21 will not start. — [xgamingserver guide](https://xgamingserver.com/blog/how-to-update-your-minecraft-server-to-26-2-java-bedrock/)
- Shockbyte's modded hosting page says its dashboard has a one-click installer for CurseForge, FTB, Technic and ATLauncher packs. — [Shockbyte modded hosting](https://shockbyte.com/games/modded-minecraft-server-hosting)

### Inferences
- A CurseForge-created server is only as current as the modpack's server pack: for a 26.3 server you would have to pick a 26.3 modpack with a published server pack. No public 26.3 Fabric modpack with a server pack is confirmed in these sources.
- Because the server lands in the regular Shockbyte panel, a teacher could in principle buy an Iron server from any modpack, then create a Fabric 26.3 instance and SFTP in custom mod jars and datapack zips. That path is undocumented by CurseForge; the "Join"/share-link convenience would then point friends at the wrong modpack, and support may push back. Treat it as unsupported.
- Datapacks (zips in `world/datapacks/`) are plain file uploads, so they work anywhere SFTP works; nothing in the CurseForge flow handles them.
- Whether Shockbyte's instance selector already lists 26.3 and a Java 25 runtime is unverified; Shockbyte normally adds new versions quickly, but confirm before relying on it.

### Gaps
- No official statement that Fabric Loader or vanilla 26.3 is selectable through the CurseForge app flow (it is modpack-first; loader is implied by the pack).
- No confirmation that the Shockbyte panel offered to CurseForge buyers has the same Instances/custom-jar/SFTP features as a direct Shockbyte account (the support article says "console, files, restart, backups, etc.").
- No Shockbyte article confirming 26.3 or Java 25 availability found (search returned only third-party guides).

## Q3. Multiple servers per account, cost per extra server, multi-world or proxy (Velocity/BungeeCord) support, dedicated IP

### Takeaway
Nothing in the beta is designed for several servers at once: each server is its own Tebex subscription at the plan price (so n small games = n × $12/month minimum), the "My Servers" list implies multiple servers are allowed, and there is no mention of multi-world, proxies, or dedicated IPs. Shockbyte's direct product offers a $5/month dedicated-IP add-on (possibly stale figure) and up to 3 stored instances per server, but only one instance runs at a time.

### Cited Findings
- The app has a "My Servers" view with a "Host A Server" button and per-server options (share, manage, subscription), i.e. a list, not a single-server slot. — [CurseForge support](https://support.curseforge.com/support/solutions/articles/9000278741-setting-up-a-curseforge-minecraft-server)
- Each plan is "billed monthly" at $12/$24/$36 and plan changes are not possible during beta; "gifting servers" and custom plans are planned. — [CurseForge support](https://support.curseforge.com/support/solutions/articles/9000278741-setting-up-a-curseforge-minecraft-server); [CurseForge blog](https://blog.curseforge.com/the-easiest-way-to-play-modded-minecraft-with-your-friends-curseforge-servers-are-here/)
- Shockbyte stores up to 3 server instances per server by default, switched with a "Switch" button (only one active). — [Shockbyte KB: Fabric](https://shockbyte.com/help/knowledgebase/articles/how-to-install-fabric-on-your-minecraft-server)
- Shockbyte dedicated IP: "the default Minecraft port (25565 …) requires the Dedicated IP option," bought in the client area under upgrades; TechRadar's review puts the add-on at $5/month (review, possibly stale). — [Shockbyte KB: Dedicated IP](https://shockbyte.com/billing/knowledgebase/6/How-to-Purchase-a-Dedicated-IP-Default-Port.html); [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/reviews/shockbyte)
- Shockbyte default is a shared IP with a port; dedicated IP is a paid add-on. — [minecraft-hosting.top, 2026-02-13](https://minecraft-hosting.top/2026/02/13/shockbyte-minecraft-hosting/)
- For comparison, BisectHosting's current "BisectOne" plan includes "2 instance slots" with a 40 GB shared storage pool and a paid add-on that adds 2 more instance slots; prices are rendered client-side (placeholders only). — [BisectHosting Minecraft hosting page](https://www.bisecthosting.com/minecraft-server-hosting)

### Inferences
- Several simultaneous games = several purchases. Three small games on Iron = $36/month; three on Gold = $72/month. A single Diamond ($36, 12 GB) cannot be split into several concurrent servers through the CurseForge flow.
- Multi-world inside one server (e.g. Multiverse-style) is a plugin/mod concern, not something the host provides; a Fabric server could do it with a mod, but the CurseForge flow gives no help.
- Velocity/BungeeCord proxies are not mentioned anywhere in the CurseForge or Shockbyte material found; running one would mean buying an extra server and configuring it manually, which defeats the point of this product.
- Dedicated IP availability for CurseForge-bought servers is unknown; the Shockbyte client area add-on may or may not be reachable through a Tebex-billed subscription.

### Gaps
- No stated maximum number of servers per CurseForge account.
- No per-additional-server discount; no bundle pricing.
- No information on whether CurseForge-bought servers can buy Shockbyte add-ons (dedicated IP, extra backups) at all.

## Q4. Operational features: console, scheduled restarts, backups, whitelist/ops, subdomain, DDoS, uptime

- Scheduled restarts are a standard Shockbyte panel feature (scheduled tasks) per general reviews, but no CurseForge-specific confirmation exists.
- Subdomain: Shockbyte-direct customers get a `*.shockbyte` style address or IP:port; nothing says CurseForge servers get a friendly subdomain (friends connect through the app link).

## Q5. Restrictions and gotchas for a classroom: age, slot caps, CPU throttling, overselling complaints

### Takeaway
The buyer must be 16+ under Tebex terms (so the teacher buys, not students), the servers run on shared vCPUs with RAM-based sizing (CurseForge's own guidance tops Iron at 1–5 players), plan changes are impossible during the beta, and the share link/import code is the only built-in access control. Shockbyte's budget tiers draw recurring lag complaints under load.

### Cited Findings
- Tebex Condition 9.1: "You may only purchase Products from the Webstore if you are at least 16 years old." — [Tebex Terms & Conditions](https://checkout.tebex.io/terms)
- CurseForge's own sizing: Iron for 1–5 players/light mods, Gold 6–15, Diamond 16–25; RAM scales with mod count. — [CurseForge support](https://support.curseforge.com/support/solutions/articles/9000278741-setting-up-a-curseforge-minecraft-server)
- No plan up/downgrade in beta. — [CurseForge blog](https://blog.curseforge.com/the-easiest-way-to-play-modded-minecraft-with-your-friends-curseforge-servers-are-here/)
- Shockbyte "Cons" (Feb 2026): "Possible lag, TPS drops, and errors under heavy load or with many players," "Budget-tier plans may be less reliable under load," "Restrictive refund policy and strict time windows." — [minecraft-hosting.top, 2026-02-13](https://minecraft-hosting.top/2026/02/13/shockbyte-minecraft-hosting/)
- Shockbyte servers are "shared CPU" on AMD EPYC / Intel Xeon nodes; no per-plan CPU figures published. — [minecraft-hosting.top, 2026-02-13](https://minecraft-hosting.top/2026/02/13/shockbyte-minecraft-hosting/)
- Two separate accounts (billing vs. game panel) is a noted inconvenience at Shockbyte; support is English-only. — [WebsitePlanet Shockbyte review](https://www.websiteplanet.com/web-hosting/shockbyte/) (possibly stale)
- Tebex: transactions are final except where consumer law requires; EU buyers get 14 days. — [Tebex Terms & Conditions](https://checkout.tebex.io/terms)
- The CurseForge app must be installed and logged in to create or join via link; the link auto-installs the app for recipients who lack it. — [CurseForge blog](https://blog.curseforge.com/the-easiest-way-to-play-modded-minecraft-with-your-friends-curseforge-servers-are-here/)

### Inferences
- For 10–20 kids on custom (light) Fabric games, CurseForge's own table points at Gold ($24, 8 GB) or Diamond ($36, 12 GB) per server, not Iron, even though a lightly modded 26.3 server would usually run 20 players in 4–6 GB; the beta's no-resize rule means you must guess right up front.
- Classroom machines would need the CurseForge app (an Overwolf desktop app, Windows/macOS; Linux via a separate alpha) to use the Join/share flow; plain Minecraft launcher or Prism clients can still connect to the IP:port like any server, but then the CurseForge layer adds nothing.
- The 20% creator payout and the Tebex merchant-of-record arrangement mean receipts come from Tebex Limited (UK), which matters for school reimbursement paperwork.

### Gaps
- No documented hard player-slot caps (CurseForge's numbers are recommendations, not limits).
- No published CPU-throttling policy or fair-use rules for CurseForge-bought servers.
- No evidence yet of CurseForge-Servers-specific overselling complaints (the product is six weeks old); Shockbyte's general history is the only proxy.
